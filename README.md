# Ecommerce Sales Performance Dashboard

## Overview

This project analyzes ecommerce sales performance using an interactive dashboard designed to monitor revenue, orders, product performance, marketing effectiveness, and fulfillment operations.

## Objectives

* Track overall business performance through key KPIs.
* Identify revenue trends over time.
* Evaluate product category performance.
* Assess payment method preferences.
* Measure marketing channel effectiveness.
* Identify operational bottlenecks affecting order fulfillment.

## Key Metrics

| Metric                    | Value  |
| ------------------------- | ------ |
| Total Revenue             | $1.26M |
| Average Revenue per Order | $1.05K |
| Total Orders              | 1,200  |
| Delivered Orders          | 231    |
| Total Quantity Sold       | 3,535  |

## Key Insights

### Revenue Trend Analysis

Revenue showed a significant decline over the three-year period:

| Year | Revenue  |
| ---- | -------- |
| 2023 | $552.64K |
| 2024 | $480.24K |
| 2025 | $231.88K |

Revenue decreased by 13.1% between 2023 and 2024 and further declined by 51.7% in 2025, indicating a need for deeper investigation into customer retention, acquisition, and operational efficiency.

### Product Category Performance

* Chairs and Printers generated the highest revenue.
* Revenue distribution across categories remained relatively balanced.
* Phones contributed the lowest revenue among the categories analyzed.

### Payment Method Analysis

* Credit Cards and Online Payments were the most utilized payment methods.
* Debit Card transactions represented the smallest share but remained significant.

### Marketing Channel Performance

* Instagram generated the highest revenue among marketing channels.
* Email, Google, and Facebook delivered comparable performance.
* Referral traffic generated the lowest revenue contribution.

### Coupon Code Performance

* FREESHIP recorded the highest number of orders.
* A large number of customers purchased without any coupon.
* Shipping incentives appear to influence customer purchasing behavior more than percentage-based discounts.

### Operational Findings

A critical finding was the low order delivery completion rate. Out of 1,200 total orders, only 231 were marked as delivered, highlighting potential fulfillment, logistics, or supply chain challenges.

## Recommendations

1. Investigate fulfillment and delivery processes to improve order completion rates.
2. Perform customer retention and cohort analysis to understand the revenue decline.
3. Expand free-shipping initiatives to improve conversion rates and customer acquisition.
4. Optimize marketing spend by leveraging high-performing channels such as Instagram.
